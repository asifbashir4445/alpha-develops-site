Features

Built like a beast. Feels like a heartbeat.

The Heart Button

One signature glowing green heart does it all: tap for video, long-press for audio only. Paste or share a link into the app, press the heart, done.

Quality picker

Where a platform offers more than one quality, you choose — crisp HD for the big screen, a light file for a small phone, or audio only. YouTube links, including YouTube Music, ride the same heart.

Alpha Vault

A private vault inside the app, locked with your PIN, password or pattern — fingerprint unlock too. What you keep in the Vault stays in the Vault.

Alpha Player

A full built-in player: seek, ±10 seconds, speed control, shuffle and repeat, a proper queue, subtitles and a sleep timer — and a screen that stays awake while you watch.

The Reveal screen

While a download runs, the video's real thumbnail grows piece by piece — true progress you can watch, finished with a golden crown the moment it hits 100%.

Parallel + Wi-Fi-only

Download several files at once, pause and resume any of them, and switch on Wi-Fi-only mode so mobile data is never touched without your say.
