The Studio

From a small village, to the world.

Alpha Develops is the studio of Asif Bashir Khan — an indie developer from Koria Balochan Wala, a small village in Bhakkar, Pakistan, whose name the world had never heard. He decided the world would hear it from his apps.

Every Alpha app carries the village's name with pride — not as a footnote, but as a flag. Because talent is not born in big cities only. It is born wherever a young person decides to begin.

Time Burn launched on Google Play in September 2026. Alpha Pulse followed — bigger, bolder — and in November 2026 it was approved and published on Google Play too, joining Time Burn there, with this site as its second door.
