Features

Why a candle?

Because time you can see is time you respect. Time Burn is built around one simple, honest picture.

Live countdowns

Birthdays, exams, launches, homecomings — set the date and watch its candle burn down live, to the second.

Goal candles

Give a goal a deadline and it becomes a candle of its own. As the wax melts, the urgency becomes something you can feel — a quiet, daily push.

Home-screen presence

Your candles stay with you on the home screen, so the time that matters is never buried inside an app you forget to open.

Progress you can see

Every candle shows how much time is gone and how much is left — a melt line that tells the truth at a glance.

Made to be gentle

No noise, no guilt trips. Just a calm flame and an honest picture of your time — motivation that feels like light, not pressure.

From Koria, with pride

Built by one developer in Koria Balochan Wala, Bhakkar — and carried in the app itself, so the village's name travels with every candle.
