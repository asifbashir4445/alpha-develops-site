Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes — free, no signup and no limits for normal use. If you download on your phone every day, the Alpha Pulse Android app is the faster home for it, with a private vault and offline playback.

Which sites work?

YouTube (videos and Shorts), Facebook, Instagram (reels and videos), TikTok, Snapchat, X, Twitch, Dailymotion, SoundCloud and many more — the same reading engine as the app. A few platforms guard their doors with logins; where a wall stands, we tell you honestly instead of pretending.

What quality can I download?

MP4 video up to 4K where the platform offers it, and audio only where you just want the sound. Qualities marked "with sound" already have audio mixed in; "video only" ones are the picture track alone.

Is it safe? Do you keep my videos?

We ask for no personal information and we never keep your video — the file passes through our server to your browser and is gone the moment it arrives. Only public videos work.

What if a link does not work?

Some videos ask the platform to sign in first (YouTube does this for certain popular uploads). Try again in a little while, or open the same link in the Alpha Pulse app, which tries more ways in.
