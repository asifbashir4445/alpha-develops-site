1 Step One

Tap “Direct Download” on the app page

Go to the Alpha Pulse or Time Burn page on this site and tap the green Direct Download (APK) button. The app file (ending in .apk) starts downloading.

4 Step Four

Turn ON “Allow from this source”

Android will say this app can't install other apps yet, and offer a Settings button. Tap it, and on the “Install unknown apps” screen turn the “Allow from this source” switch ON — for your browser or Files app, whichever you opened the file from. Then tap Back.

This permission belongs to the app doing the installing (Chrome or Files) — you're not giving it to our app. Step 7 shows how to switch it back off.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Is it safe?

Yes — as long as the file comes from this website. Every Alpha APK is signed by Alpha Develops, and Android verifies that signature during install; a tampered copy would refuse to install over ours. The Chrome warning in Step 2 appears for every direct APK on the internet, honest or not — which is exactly why the source matters. This site is the source. If someone sends you an “Alpha” APK from anywhere else, don't install it — come here instead. Still unsure? Write to us at asifbashir4445@gmail.com.

Is Alpha Pulse on Google Play?

Yes! Alpha Pulse was approved and published on Google Play in November 2026 — it is rolling out through closed testing first, and the public listing opens right after. This site stays its second door: the same signed app as a direct APK, for anyone who prefers no store in between. Time Burn lives happily on both doors too: Google Play and right here.

Will my app update itself?

Apps installed from Google Play update through the Play Store as usual. Apps you install directly from here are updated the same simple way you installed them: when a new version is announced on this site, download the new APK and install it over the old one — your settings and your Vault stay untouched. Android recognises it as the same signed app, just newer.

It says “App not installed”. What now?

Three usual causes: the download didn't finish (download it again), an older or different copy is in the way (uninstall the old copy first — note that this clears its data), or the file came from somewhere else and its signature doesn't match ours. When in doubt, delete the file, download fresh from this site, and try once more. Still stuck? Email us — we answer.
